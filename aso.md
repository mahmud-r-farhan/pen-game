## 6. Google Play Policy & Data Safety Verification

* **Data Collection:** `No data collected`. (The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal user info).
* **Data Sharing:** `No third-party data sharing`.
* **Data Encryption:** All network requests to Pexels, Pixabay, and Wallhaven use secure HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption.
* **Permissions Requested:**
  - `android.permission.SET_WALLPAPER` (Required to apply wallpapers).
  - `android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` & `android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` (Scoped storage to let users pick their own gallery videos/photos).
  - `android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` (Restores screen unlock listener after device restart).
